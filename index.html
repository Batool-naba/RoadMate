What is RoadMate?

Broken down on the road? RoadMate connects you with a verified mechanic nearby — in minutes. Send a request, watch your mechanic drive to you on a live map, chat on the way, and pay in cash when the job is done.

🔧 Help in secondsEvery available mechanic near you is notified instantly.
📍 Live trackingSee exactly where your mechanic is and how long they'll take.
🧠 Smart diagnosisNot sure what's wrong? A few questions narrow it down.
🚨 Emergency SOSOne tap for Rescue 1122, Motorway Police and more.

How to install

  1. Tap Download for Android above.
  2. When the download finishes, open the file.
  3. Android will ask permission to install from this source — tap Settings, turn it on, then go back.
  4. Tap Install, then open RoadMate and create your account.
Seeing a warning? Android shows a caution message for any app installed outside the Play Store. RoadMate is safe — the app is digitally signed. Tap Install anyway to continue.
